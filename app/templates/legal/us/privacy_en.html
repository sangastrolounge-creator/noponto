Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ L.versao.strftime('%B %d, %Y') }}

This policy explains what information {{ L.empresa }} (“{{ L.marca }}”, “we”) collects through the {{ L.marca }} website, web app and mobile app (the “Service”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

  • Businesses that sign up (our “Customers”): we decide how we use the account owner’s and managers’ contact and billing details.
  • Employees invited by an employer: your employer decides what to collect about your work and why. We process that information on your employer’s behalf, as its service provider. Questions or requests about your work records should go to your employer first. We will help them answer.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email (managers), mobile phone number (employees), business name and address, and your password, which we store only as a secure hash.
  • Work information entered by the employer or the employee: schedules, availability, clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, time-off requests and balances, job roles, pay rates and salary, tips, worker classification (W-2 or 1099), hire date, tasks, shift swaps, team-board messages and shift ratings.
  • Location at clock-in and clock-out only: when an employee clocks in or out by phone, the app reads the device’s location (latitude, longitude and accuracy) at that moment to check that they are at the workplace. Kiosk punches use the business’s tablet and a PIN, not the employee’s location.
  • On-shift area checks, only if the employer turns them on: while an employee is clocked in and has the app open on screen, the app checks the location about every 2 minutes to see whether they are inside the work area, and alerts the employer if they stay outside it. We never read location when the app is closed, in the background, or outside a shift.
  • Photos at clock-in and clock-out, if the employer turns this on: a photo is taken at the moment of the punch so the employer can confirm who clocked in. Photos are deleted automatically after the period the employer chooses, up to 45 days. We do not use face recognition and do not create face templates or other biometric identifiers from these photos.
  • Device information: a random identifier stored in a cookie on the device and the browser’s name. Together they recognize the device used to clock in and alert the employer if a new device is used. We also store push-notification subscriptions if you allow notifications.
  • Payment information: card payments are handled by Stripe. We receive the subscription status and the amounts paid. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Technical information: IP address and request logs. We use them for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide the Service: showing schedules, recording time, calculating hours and estimated pay, sending reminders and alerts, and producing reports and exports. We also use it to secure accounts and prevent fraud, to provide support, to bill Customers, to send service messages such as password resets and important changes, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not show ads.

4. Who we share it with

  • The employer: employee information is visible to the employer’s account owner and to managers who have permission for that area.
  • Service providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of the data:
    • cloud hosting, where the application and database run;
    • Stripe, for payments;
    • our email provider, for password-reset and service emails;
    • Anthropic, our AI provider, only when a manager asks for an automated pay-period review. Data sent through Anthropic’s commercial API is not used to train its models.
    • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces on our pages and receives your IP address when it delivers them;
    • OpenStreetMap, which supplies the map tiles when a manager draws a work-area fence, and receives that manager’s IP address;
    • an off-site storage provider that keeps our backups. Backups are encrypted on our server before they are sent.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of people or of the Service.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Where data is stored

Our servers are located in {{ L.pais }}. If you use the Service from the United States or elsewhere, your information is transferred to and processed in that country, with the safeguards described in this policy.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and work records are kept while the Customer’s account is active. Federal law generally requires employers to keep payroll records for 3 years and time cards for 2 years. Your employer controls these records.
  • Clock-in photos are deleted after the employer’s chosen period, never more than 45 days. The time record itself remains.
  • When an account is closed, we keep its data for 90 days so the Customer can ask for an export, and then delete it. Copies in our encrypted backups are overwritten within about 120 days.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, permission-based access for managers, and encrypted off-site backups. Access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will notify the affected Customer and, where required, the affected people.

8. Cookies

We use only cookies that the Service needs to work: a sign-in session, the device identifier described above, and your language and light/dark theme preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Employees: you can see your schedule, punches and hours in the app. To correct a time record, request an adjustment in the app or talk to your employer. You can refuse location or photo collection, but your employer may then require you to clock in at the kiosk or by another method.
  • Notifications: you can turn push notifications off in your device or browser settings.
  • State privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. Requests about work records are handled with your employer. You can also write to us at the address below, and we will verify your request and respond within the time the law requires. You may use an authorized agent.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and their workers. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Employers that hire minors are responsible for following youth-employment laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change is material, we will tell account owners by email or in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
